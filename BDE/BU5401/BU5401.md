# BU5401: Business Analytics

## Course Overview
This course teaches how to use analytical tools and Excel to solve business problems. The content is valuable but requires significant time commitment.

## Learning Experience
- Course material is engaging and practical
- Professor provides comprehensive real-world examples
- YouTube videos by the professor are informative but lengthy
- Pre-studying before class is highly recommended for better understanding

## Skills Developed
- Advanced Excel techniques for business problem-solving
- Data analysis methodologies
- Decision-making frameworks
- Practical business analytics applications

## Assessment Structure
- First exam: Time allocation is generally sufficient
- Second exam: Time pressure is significant, preparation for quick problem-solving is essential
- Assignment work requires consistent effort throughout the semester

- [BU5401 SAE1-7.pdf](./BU5401%20SAE1-7.pdf) - Sample exercises with solutions

## Tips for Success
- Study consistently throughout the semester
- Practice Excel exercises regularly
- Form study groups to discuss complex concepts
- Prepare cheat sheets or quick reference guides for exams
- Develop time management strategies for the second exam
