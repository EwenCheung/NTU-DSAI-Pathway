# BU5501: Marketing

## Course Overview
This course covers fundamental marketing concepts and strategies. It's relatively manageable in terms of time commitment but requires strong critical thinking and analytical skills.

## Learning Experience
- Comfortable course pacing
- Emphasis on conceptual understanding rather than memorization
- Success depends on ability to elaborate and apply concepts
- Develops analytical and critical thinking skills

## Assessment Structure
- First exam: Closed book Multiple Choice Questions
- Second exam: Open book essay format
  - Highly subjective evaluation based on depth of analysis
  - Requires comprehensive application of marketing concepts
  - Quality of elaboration is key to scoring well
