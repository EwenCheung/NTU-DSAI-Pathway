# EE5081: Technological Innovation

## Course Overview
This course focuses on technological innovation principles, entrepreneurship, and business plan development. It combines technical knowledge with business acumen to help students understand how innovations are commercialized.

## Key Topics
- Innovation Management
- Technology Strategy
- Business Model Development
- Intellectual Property Protection
- Market Analysis and Validation
- Entrepreneurial Finance
- Business Plan Creation

## Business Plan Components
The business plan is a central deliverable for this course, including:
- Executive Summary
- Company Description
- Market Analysis
- Competitive Analysis
- Product/Service Description
- Marketing and Sales Strategy
- Financial Projections
- Implementation Timeline

## Assessment Preparation
- Focus on both theoretical innovation concepts and practical application
- Review past exam questions to understand evaluation patterns
- Practice developing concise yet comprehensive business pitches
- Understand financial modeling for technology startups

## Tips for Success
- Begin working on your business plan early in the semester
- Conduct thorough market research to validate your innovation concept
- Create realistic financial projections with clear assumptions
- Develop a compelling value proposition for your innovation
- Practice presenting your business plan in a concise, engaging manner

## Industry Applications
- This course provides valuable skills for technology entrepreneurship
- Knowledge can be applied to corporate innovation roles
- Useful for students interested in startup ventures or product management
