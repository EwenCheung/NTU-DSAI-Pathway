# CC0003: Ethics and Civic Responsibility

## Course Overview
This course explores ethical principles and civic responsibility across various contexts. Active participation and verbal engagement are essential for success.

## Panel Discussion Tips
- **Schedule early**: Have your panel discussion as early as possible to avoid end-of-semester time constraints
- **Participation is key**: Speak up regularly during class to demonstrate engagement
- **Recommended tutors**: Kai Xiang and Christopher are highly recommended by previous students

## Panel Discussion Best Practices
### Strengths to Emulate
- Develop clear thesis statements with focused arguments
- Provide explicit explanations of ethical concepts (e.g., how Sex-Selective Abortion is a form of discrimination)
- Include real-world examples to illustrate key points
- Choose one ethical theory and apply it consistently throughout your presentation

### Areas for Improvement
- Include proper in-text citations to support your arguments
- Define all ethical terms and theories you reference (e.g., 'virtue ethics')
- Explicitly connect rebuttals and counter-arguments back to the specific topic of the question

## Individual Viva Preparation
- Evidence and examples are crucial for a strong performance
- Prepare concrete cases that demonstrate your understanding of ethical principles
- Practice articulating your reasoning clearly and concisely
- Be prepared to respond to challenging questions about your ethical positions
