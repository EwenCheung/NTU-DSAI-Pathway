# CC0002: Navigating Complexity with Systems Thinking

## Course Overview
This course focuses on systems thinking approaches to understand and navigate complex problems across various disciplines. The final exam is the most critical component for achieving a high grade.

## Grade Determinants
- **Exam performance is crucial**
  - A strong exam score typically results in an A grade
  - Average exam performance typically results in a B+ grade
- Other assessment components have less impact on the final grade

## Key Topics
- Systems thinking methodology
- Complex adaptive systems
- Feedback loops and system dynamics
- Interdisciplinary problem-solving
- Modeling complex systems
