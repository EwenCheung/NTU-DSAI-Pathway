# CC0005: Healthy Living and Well-being

## Course Overview
This course focuses on physical and mental well-being concepts, with distinct components for Well Being (WB) and Healthy Living (HL).

**Recommended tutors**: Norman New are highly recommended by previous students

## Component Comparison
### Well Being (WB)
- Generally easier to score well in
- Focus on theoretical understanding and articulation of well-being concepts
- Assessment typically through presentations and written work
- [CC5 WL presentationSlide.pdf](./CC5%20WL%20presentationSlide.pdf) provides a good reference template

### Healthy Lifestyle (HL)
- More challenging component requiring program creation
- Requires development of a practical wellness program
- Assessment based on program design and implementation results

## Presentation Guidelines
- Create concise, visual slides rather than text-heavy content
- Use graphics, charts, and images to illustrate key points
- Keep text minimal with focus on key takeaways
- Practice delivery to ensure clarity and engagement

## Program Development (HL Component)
### Key Requirements
- **Clearly defined focus**: Specific health or wellness objectives
- **Structured tracking**: Measurable parameters and progress indicators
- **Implementation plan**: Realistic timeline and actionable steps
- **Follow-up mechanisms**: How progress will be monitored
- **Results analysis**: Evaluation of program effectiveness
- **Concept**: Use the concept learn in class while presenting

## Tips for Success
- Begin program planning early to allow for implementation period
- Select realistic, measurable goals for your wellness program
- Document progress systematically with evidence
- Use existing health apps or tools to support tracking
- Create a visually appealing presentation of your program and results

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Overly ambitious or vague program goals
- Insufficient tracking mechanisms
- Lack of clear results or outcome measurements
- Text-heavy presentations that lose audience interest
