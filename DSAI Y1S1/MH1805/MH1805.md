# MH1805: Calculus

## Course Overview
This course covers fundamental concepts of calculus including differentiation, integration, and their applications in data science and engineering problems.

## Continuous Assessment
- Group projects are a significant component of the assessment
- Active participation in group work directly affects peer evaluation scores
- High peer evaluation scores can boost your overall grade above 100%
- Strategies for success:
  - Contribute actively to group discussions and problem-solving
  - Take initiative in project work
  - Ensure equitable distribution of tasks
  - Communicate clearly with team members

## Key Topics
- Limits and Continuity
- Differentiation and Applications
- Integration Techniques
- Series and Sequences
- Multivariable Calculus
- Differential Equations

## Exam Preparation
- Practice with past year papers is highly effective
- Focus on understanding problem-solving techniques rather than memorization
- Master standard calculus problems and their variations
- Review tutorial questions and examples from lectures

## Common Challenging Areas
- Applications of integration
- Series convergence tests
- Multivariable optimization problems
- Vector calculus concepts
- Differential equations
