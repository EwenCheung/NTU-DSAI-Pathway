# MH1812: Discrete Mathematics

## Course Overview
This course covers fundamental concepts in discrete mathematics that form the foundation for computer science and data science applications. Topics include logic, set theory, combinatorics, graph theory, and number theory.

## Exam Preparation
- **Focus on understanding all core concepts thoroughly**
- Practice with past year papers is highly effective
- Pay special attention to proof techniques and applications
- Work through all tutorial problems until you can solve them independently

## Common Challenging Areas
- Mathematical induction
- Proof by contradiction
- Graph theory applications
- Recurrence relations
- Combinatorial proofs
- No mistakes allowed for CA (else at most get A-)

## Study Resources
- Course textbook and lecture notes
- Practice problems from tutorials
- Past year examination papers
- YouTube Organic Chemistry Tutor

## Tips for Success
- Build strong foundations in the basic concepts before moving to more complex topics
- Practice writing clear, step-by-step proofs
- Form study groups to discuss challenging problems
- Create summary sheets for key theorems and formulas
- Solve diverse problem types to prepare for exam variations
